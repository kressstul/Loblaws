Best,

## 3) Customer cannot log in (troubleshooting)

### Customer Response
Hi [Customer Name],  
Thanks for reaching out. I am sorry you are running into login trouble. I am happy to help.

Please try these steps:
1. Confirm you are using the exact email tied to your Levitate account.
2. Click **Forgot Password?** and check spam/junk if the reset email does not appear.
3. Try an incognito/private window, clear cache/cookies, or use another supported browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge).
4. If your invite expired or access was removed, your Account Admin may need to reactivate your user.
5. If your organization uses SSO, confirm you are using the correct SSO flow and active identity provider credentials.

If it still fails, please send:
- The email you are using
- A screenshot of the error
- The approximate time of the failed attempt

I will review this immediately and escalate if needed.

Best,  
Krystal

### Internal Actions
**Before sending:** Confirm user status (active/deactivated/locked/pending), check password reset delivery and SSO/authentication flags, identify admin contacts.  
**After sending:** Monitor diagnostics, validate login/reset events, escalate for auth or SSO issues, and confirm successful login.

## 4) Double charge complaint with refund demand

### Customer Response
Hi [Customer Name],  
Thank you for flagging this, and I am very sorry for the frustration. I understand why seeing two charges is upsetting.

I have escalated this to Billing as a priority. We are reviewing invoices and payment activity now to confirm whether this was a duplicate charge, retry, renewal, or another billing event.

What you can expect next:
- Update within 30-60 minutes with confirmed findings
- Clear explanation of the billing event
- If duplicate is confirmed, immediate refund/reversal submission with confirmation

If a refund is issued, bank posting times can vary, and I will share expected timing so you know what to expect.

I will own this through resolution and keep communication clear and prompt.

Best,  
Krystal

### Internal Actions
**Before sending:** Review invoice IDs/dates/amounts/payment method details, validate whether charges map to same billing period, evaluate common causes, align with Billing on policy and timeline.  
**After sending:** Escalate with full billing evidence, confirm root cause, provide update within promised window, complete correction, and close with clear explanation.
