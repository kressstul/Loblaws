3) Customer cannot log in (troubleshooting)

Customer Response

Hi [Customer Name],

Thanks for reaching out. I am sorry you are running into login trouble. I am happy to help.

Please try these steps:

  1. Confirm you are using the exact email tied to your Levitate account.
  2. Click Forgot Password? and check spam/junk if the reset email does not appear.
  3. Try an incognito/private window, clear cache/cookies, or use another supported browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge).
  4. If your invite expired or access was removed, your Account Admin may need to reactivate your user.
  5. If your organization uses SSO, confirm you are using the correct SSO flow and active identity provider credentials.

If it still fails, please send the email you are using, a screenshot of the error, and the approximate time of the failed attempt.

I will review this immediately and escalate if needed.

Best,
Krystal

Internal actions (before): Confirm user status, check password-reset delivery and SSO/authentication flags, identify admin contacts.
Internal actions (after): Monitor diagnostics, validate login/reset events, escalate for auth or SSO issues, and confirm successful login.

Best,
Krystal
